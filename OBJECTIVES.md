## 3. What "getting the most out of it" means

Value is measured in this order. Money matters, but it comes last.

1. **Liberty impact:** does it move a level of community toward the Regulative Principles of Liberty?
2. **Family impact:** does it help your household?
3. **Reach and reuse:** can others pick it up and use it without you?
4. **Time:** does it save your time or multiply it through teams and AI?
5. **Money:** what does it cost, and is there a cheaper way to the same result?
