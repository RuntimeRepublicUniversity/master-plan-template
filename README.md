# Master Plan Starter Kit

**Map your life before you ask to work with anyone else.**

This kit helps you build your own **master plan**: what you are living for, which projects serve it, and which tools (AI, GitHub, websites, domains, hosting) serve those projects. It also records the **minimum values** every member of a Runtime Republic team holds: the Regulative Principles of Liberty.

Runtime Republic University (RRU) · Draft. Nothing here is final.

## Why start here

Teams only work when the people in them share a few rules. One rule matters most: **nobody gets to capture the group.** The simplest test is to ask everyone, starting with the founder, to put their own plan in writing, versioned and open to review. People who want to serve can do that. People who want to rule rarely will.

## How to start (Station 0)

1. Click **Use this template** → **Create a new repository**.
2. Owner: **your own GitHub account**. Name: `master-plan`. Visibility: **Private** (your personal goals are yours).
3. Connect the repo to your AI assistant (a Claude Project, ChatGPT Project, Grok Project or Cursor).
4. Work through the three files **in this order**:

| Order | File | What you write |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [OBJECTIVES.md](OBJECTIVES.md) | Why: your goals for your family and for each level of community |
| 2 | [CONTROL-01-Values.md](CONTROL-01-Values.md) | The minimum values: adopt RP1 to RP6 with one commit |
| 3 | [CONTROL-00-Master-Plan.md](CONTROL-00-Master-Plan.md) | What and with what: your projects, repos, AI tools, websites, domains and hosting, each mapped to an objective |

**Prompt to give your AI:**

> Read my master-plan repo. Interview me one question at a time, with multiple-choice answers, to fill in OBJECTIVES.md first, then CONTROL-00-Master-Plan.md. Do not change CONTROL-01-Values.md except to add my name and date when I say I adopt it.

## Your plan stays private. Your values do not.

When you join a team, you add your name and the date of your CONTROL 01 adoption to that team's `MEMBERS.md` by pull request. That pull request is your public word that you hold RP1 to RP6. Your fruits do the rest (CONTROL 01, Section 4).
