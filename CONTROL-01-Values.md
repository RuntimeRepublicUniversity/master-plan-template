These are the **minimum values** for working on any Runtime Republic team. They are the same for everyone, founders included. Do not edit the principles in your copy; adopt them or don't. If you think one is wrong, say so openly in the framework repo, where it can be answered in public.

## 1. Chief premise

**Every nation has Christ as King. Whether a nation enjoys Christ as King depends on whether its government system adheres to the Regulative Principles of Liberty.**

"He maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust" (Matthew 5:45). These principles are designed the same way: to bless all people.

## 2. The six principles

Scripture is read from the King James Bible (KJB) first.

| # | Principle | KJB | Test question |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP1 | **God makes the rules.** Rulers are ministers, not owners. | Romans 13:4 | What higher source does this power trace to, and what good does it serve? |
| RP2 | **What is authorized is permitted; what is not authorized is forbidden to government.** Silence restricts. | Deuteronomy 4:2 | Where, in writing, is this power granted? |
| RP3 | **No elite capture.** No official, board or majority adds binding requirements beyond the written standard. | Genesis 3:3; Matthew 15:9 | Who added this, and under what written authority? |
| RP4 | **Rain on the just and the unjust.** The same rule for everyone, rich or poor, connected or not. | Matthew 5:45; Leviticus 19:15; Deuteronomy 1:17 | Would this rule treat the next person, of any station, the same way? |
| RP5 | **Enumerate, limit, protect.** Every measure lists and limits the power it uses, and protects life, liberty and property. | Exodus 20:13, 15; Leviticus 25:10 | Does it pass both parts? |
| RP6 | **Remember.** History is read through Scripture, and each generation teaches it to the next. | Psalm 78:5–11 | What has history already shown, and are we teaching it? |

## 3. My adoption

That commit, with its date, is your record. When you join a team, add your name and this date to the team's `MEMBERS.md` by pull request.

## 4. Known by their fruits

"Ye shall know them by their fruits" (Matthew 7:16). Words are not the gate; conduct is. These are the warning signs of someone trying to capture a group. Watch for them in yourself first.

| Principle | Warning sign |
|---|---|
| RP1 | Treats the team, its followers or its data as theirs to own |
| RP2 | Asks for admin rights or decision power before contributing any work |
| RP3 | Adds rules for others, or decides things in private messages instead of public issues |
| RP4 | Exempts themselves or their friends; treats members by status |
| RP5 | Will not put their own plan in writing, or blocks others from leaving |
| RP6 | Dismisses the record and history; wants to start over on their own terms |

## 5. How trust is earned

1. **Your own plan first:** OBJECTIVES, this CONTROL, and CONTROL 00 in your own repo.
2. **Small pull requests:** your first work is reviewed in public.
3. **Team membership:** your name in the team's `MEMBERS.md`.
4. **Authority last:** any authority is written down, time-limited and renewable.

## 6. Guard on the gatekeepers

The gate must pass RP1 to RP6 itself, or the gatekeepers become the elite:

- **Same written criteria for everyone**, founders included.
- **Public decisions:** admissions and removals are recorded in the team repo.
- **Appeal:** anyone can ask for a decision to be reviewed in public.
- **Exit:** anyone can leave freely and take their own work with them.
- **Lawful process:** removal happens only by recorded process, never by private action (AGCL 000, Rule S7).
